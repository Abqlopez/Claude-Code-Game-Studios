## Different from 2D on purpose
- 6 tables is a dock patio, not a bigger boat.
- Build mode moves tables only (kitchen stations stay where they are).
- Every fish has its own bait and lure, and nothing bites without it (2D let any bait work, just slower).
- Seating works like 2D (greet, lead, seat), and fights have moods, pulls, a late trick and a landing bar.
