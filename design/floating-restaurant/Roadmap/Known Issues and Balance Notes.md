# Known issues and balance notes (v4.1)
- **Bait is now required**, so a menu fish you have no bait for can't be caught until you buy it that afternoon. Watch how this feels on days 2-4.
- Guests and staff do not collide with each other (they follow set paths around the tables); guests you lead walk straight toward you.
- Only one cutting board in 3D even with the Kitchen upgrade (2D gives a second board).
- Build mode only has room for small moves with 4 tables (they must stay a table apart).
- A new game starts with a plain boat (no lanterns until bought), so the first nights are darker than v4.
- Patience, cook times, arrival speed, fish move difficulty, mood weights and landing-bar speed still need tuning from real play.
- On a very slow device a quick tap of SPACE can nudge a hold bar forward a little (the hold uses frame time).
