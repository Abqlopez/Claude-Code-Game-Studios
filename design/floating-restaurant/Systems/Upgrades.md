# Upgrades

| Upgrade | Prices | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Tables | $150, $350 | 4, then 6 tables. The boat grows with them |
| Fish tank | $120, $300 | Holds 15, then 25. Fish stay fresh 1 day longer. Only level 2 keeps a boss fish overnight |
| Kitchen | $200, $450 | A 2nd cutting board and 2, then 3 burners per station |
| Menu board | $200 | 6 menu slots instead of 4 |
| Big tables | $120, $260 | Table 2, then table 4, seats 2-4. Groups and Families only book if you have one |
| VIP table | $400 | Ropes off table 1. Royals only book (and only sit) at a VIP table |
| Dragon Grill | $350 | A far hotter grill. The Dragon Koi Feast cannot be cooked without it |
| Boat engine | $1500 | Unlocks sailing to Coral Bay |

In 3D (v4): Tables, Fish tank, Kitchen, Menu board, Dragon Grill and Boat engine are in the Upgrades tab. Tables stop at 4 for now (6 tables come with a bigger boat); Big tables and the VIP table are not in 3D yet. The engine unlocks Coral Bay on the "Where to fish today?" screen.

## In 3D (v4.1)
- **Tables:** level 1 = 4 tables on deck; level 2 = **2 more on a dock patio** (6 in all), instead of a longer boat.
- **Big tables:** table 2, then table 4, get 4 seats. **VIP table:** gold ropes around table 1; Royals only book with it.
- **Boat engine:** unlocks Coral Bay on the sea chart and moving the restaurant there (Upgrades → Move the restaurant, $40). Where the restaurant is moored decides which guests book.
