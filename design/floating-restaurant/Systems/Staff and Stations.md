# Staff and stations (v4.1)

Every member of staff except the Deckhand needs a **station**. Build a station, then hire one person for it; up to 2 of each.

| Station | Build | Staff | Hire | Wage/night | Where | What they do |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Host stand | $150 | Host | $300 | $20 | on the dock | Seats guests at the right table (keeps BIG and VIP tables for those who need them) and collects their bills |
| Waiter station | $150 | Waiter | $500 | $30 | by the pass | Takes orders and serves any dish left on the PASS |
| Dish pit | $200 | Dishwasher | $250 | $15 | kitchen | Clears tables, washes plates |
| Cook station | $300 | Line Cook | $800 | $40 | kitchen island | Fetches, chops, cooks on its own stove, leaves dishes on the pass |
| (none) | — | Deckhand | $1000 | $40 | your skiff | Fishes all morning: about 4 / 6 / 8 fish at level 1 / 2 / 3, using your bait |

- **Training:** level 2 costs $150, level 3 $300. Speed 100% / 120% / 140%. Staff tire late in the night (up to 25% slower, less when trained).
- **Moods:** wages are paid after closing. Miss one and they get grumpy and work slower until paid again.
- With a Waiter you can leave a dish on the pass instead of carrying it out yourself.
