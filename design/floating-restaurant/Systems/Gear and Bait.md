# Gear, bait and lures (v4.1)

## The rule
**Every fish bites only its own bait, or its own lure. No bait, no bite.** That goes for the rod, the Net, the Deckhand, and night fishing off the bow. (Crab and Lobster still come from [[Crab Traps]].)

- **Discovering bait:** cast at a fish with the wrong bait and it swims off, but tells you exactly what it wants. Its bait and lure then show up in the shop. Bait you have not discovered stays hidden.
- **R** puts the right bait (or its lure, if you own one) on for the sign in front of you. **Q** cycles through everything you carry.
- **Your bait draws its fish:** new signs on the water are 3x more likely to be the fish your hook is for. Signs you know that will take your bait get a green ring.
- Bait is used up one per cast. A fish on its own bait starts 30% tired.
- **Your menu's needs** are shown on the Guests tab, at the start of the morning and in the journal, with your bait count for each fish.
- A new game starts with 8 Krill and 6 Glow paste (for the starting menu), and 3 Sardines and 3 Glowgills already in the tank.
- Crabfolk pay in shells: any bait you know costs half its price in shells (at least 1). Pirates' treasure maps and Sea Kings' tips give Gold flakes.

## Lure perks
| Perk | Level I | Level II |
|---|---|---|
| tired | It starts 25% tired. | It starts 45% tired. |
| quick | It bites almost at once, with a longer window. | It bites at once, with an even longer window. |
| big | Bigger fish bite it. | Much bigger fish bite it. |
| clean | Forgives 1 mistake, so clean catches are easier. | Forgives 2 mistakes. |

A lure counts as that fish's bait and is never used up.
