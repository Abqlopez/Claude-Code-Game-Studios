# Sea Witch blessings and curses
A Sea Witch who reviews you 4+ leaves a blessing; 2 or less leaves a curse, for the whole next day.

| Hex | Good? | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Lucky Tides | yes | Bigger fish in the morning. |
| Golden Touch | yes | Tips are 30% bigger at night. |
| Calm Crowd | yes | Customers are 25% more patient. |
| Butterfingers | no | Plates break 4 times as often. |
| Restless Crowd | no | Customers are 20% less patient. |
| Shy Fish | no | Schools swim off much sooner in the morning. |

(The 3D service does not hand out hexes yet; hex effects already apply if one is active.)
