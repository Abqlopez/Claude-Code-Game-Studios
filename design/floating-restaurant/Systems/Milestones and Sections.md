# Milestones and boat sections (v3 step 5)

| Milestone | Reward |
|---|---|
| Earn $1,500 in total | Unlocks the Bar |
| Serve every kind of guest in Home Waters | Unlocks the Sushi counter |
| Reach 4.5 stars | $200 |
| Earn $10,000 in total | $500  (new waters arrive in v4) |

| Section | Price | Needs | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bar | $300 | earn1500 | Drinks on the house: complaint drinks are free and every guest is 10% more patient |
| Sushi counter | $450 | species | A new station. No-cook dishes (kelp wrap, sushi) made on it are always Perfect |

Sections are 2D only so far.

## In 3D (v4.1)
The **Build** tab shows sections and milestones with progress.
- **Bar** (at the bow): free drinks for complaints and +10% patience for every guest.
- **Sushi counter** (at the bow): carry a chopped no-cook dish there and HOLD to roll it: always Perfect.
- **Build mode:** move the 4 deck tables (click one, click where it goes). They stay either side of the middle aisle and at least a table apart. Reset puts them back.
