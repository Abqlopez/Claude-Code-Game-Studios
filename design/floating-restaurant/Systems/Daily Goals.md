# Daily goals (v3 step 4)
One morning goal and two night goals each day. Rewards are cash, or (15% of the time) a free decor item up to $60.

**Morning:** Catch a {fish} ($25); Catch {n} fish ($20); Land {n} clean catches ($25); Catch a Big or Trophy fish ($25)

**Night:** Serve {n} {who} guests ($25); Serve {n} {who} guests ($25); Serve {n} Perfect dishes ($30); Reach a combo of {n} ($25); Nobody walks out ($30); Earn ${n} in tips ($25)

Shown in the HUD in both 3D and 2D; listed as done or missed on the results screen.
