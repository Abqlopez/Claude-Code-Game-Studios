# Guests

Each guest has a **role** (how they behave) and a **species** (what they eat).

## Roles
| Role | Weight | Patience | Pays | From day | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sailor | 45 | high (85 s) | x1 | 1 | cheap + fast |
| Foodie | 30 | medium (66 s) | x1.2 | 1 | wants quality |
| Pirate | 25 | low (50 s) | x1.6 | 1 | priciest dish |
| Family | 10 | high (85 s) | x1 | 2 | 3-4 people, BIG table |
| Influencer | 10 | medium (66 s) | x1 | 2 | happy = more guests tomorrow |
| Critic | 7 | medium (66 s) | x1 | 3 | in disguise! review counts x5 |
| Royal | 6 | high (85 s) | x1.5 | 4 | 3 courses, one at a time |

## Species
| Species | Eats | Pays | From day | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Human | real, fantasy, kelp, mixed | x1 | 1 | nervous about fantasy fish |
| Mermaid | kelp | x1 | 1 | kelp dishes only |
| Sea King | fantasy | x1.3 | 1 | fantasy fish only |
| Octofolk | real, fantasy, kelp, mixed | x1 | 1 | orders 2 dishes |
| Crabfolk | real, fantasy, kelp, mixed | x1 | 2 | cheapest dish, pays in shells |
| Sea Witch | fantasy | x1 | 3 | fantasy only. Blesses or curses |

## Regulars
- **Old Gus** (human sailor): loves Grilled Sardine; befriend them to unlock Thunder Eel Soup. "Evenin', cap'n. The usual, if you've got it."
- **Marina** (mermaid foodie): loves Kelp Wrap; befriend them to unlock Seafood Platter. "Your deck looks lovely tonight."

## Booking
- Guests per night: 4 at 1 star up to 10 at 5 stars, +0.5 per day played, max 16.
- The list is set in the afternoon; each guest's dish is decided and its ingredients set aside when you open. The Guests tab warns what you are **SHORT ON** (v3.2).
- Families/groups need a big table; Royals need the VIP table; Critics book in disguise; Influencers bring extra guests tomorrow; Crabfolk pay in shells; Sea Witches leave a [[Sea Witch Hexes|blessing or curse]].
- Patience (seconds above) drains 0.5x as fast while waiting in the queue.

## In 3D (v4.1)
Everything above works in 3D, and every guest looks like what they are:
- **Species:** Humans (all skin tones), **Mermaids** (fish tail, shell top, long hair), **Sea Kings** (blue-green, beard, crown, red cape, trident), **Octofolk** (big purple head, tentacles), **Crabfolk** (round shell, claws, eye stalks), **Sea Witches** (green, robe, pointed hat, glowing staff).
- **Roles:** Sailors (striped shirt, sailor cap), Foodies (beret, napkin bib), Pirates (tricorn, eyepatch, red sash), Families (sun hat, with kids), Influencers (phone, pink shades, photo flashes), Critics (disguised as someone else until the bill), Royals (crown, purple robe and cape).
- **Regulars:** Old Gus (grey beard, captain's hat, pipe) and Marina (red hair, flowers) show their name and friendship hearts ♥♡♡.
- **They talk and react:** chat bubbles, questions you answer (1 or 2), moods you can see (humming, tapping, waving, standing up), complaints (apologise, free drink, remake), pirates who run for the dock, Sea Kings who demand another table, influencers taking photos, critics writing notes.
- **Seating:** greet a guest at the podium and they follow you; walk to a table and press E. Groups need a BIG table, Royals the VIP table. Guests who can't eat anything on the menu come, read it, say why and leave.
- Food you serve appears on their plates; dirty plates stay until cleared.
