# Crab traps
- Buy for $40 each, up to 3. Press T in the morning to drop one behind the boat.
- After 25 s it says READY (green on the [[Mini Map]]); sail over and press SPACE to haul it up.
- Catch: Crab 70%, Lobster 30% ([[Crab]] sometimes comes in twos). [[Lobster]] was added in v3.1.
- Traps still out when the morning ends are hauled in if they are ready.
