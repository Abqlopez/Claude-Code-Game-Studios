# Money and pricing (v2 rules)
- Start with $100, 5 plates, and these recipes: [[Grilled Sardine]], [[Glowgill Tacos]], [[Kelp Wrap]].
- **Dish price = 3x ingredient cost** (fish value + side prices) unless the recipe has a fixed price.
- Raw fish sells for 40% of what it would earn as a dish (50% of that if only "OK" fresh).
- Plates cost $12.
- Journal: a new fish pays 3x its value; a completed area journal pays $150.
- Everything goes through a ledger, so the results screen adds up: dishes sold, tips, raw fish, rewards, ingredients, bait, plates, wages, and money invested in the boat.
