## In 3D (v4.1)
- **Fishing:** each morning starts on a hand-drawn **sea chart**. Click a sea for its card: kinds of fish found, which fish you have bait for, the guests who live there, and whether a giant is rumoured. Coral Bay is locked until the Boat engine; a fogged "Deep Trench" is a teaser for later.
- **The restaurant itself** can be moved (Upgrades → Move the restaurant). Guests come from where it is moored. In Coral Bay the sea and sky change colour and coral reefs ring the boat.
