# Kitchen

Same rules as v3, rebuilt in 3D (fixed in the v4 follow-ups):

| Spot | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Fish tank** (left wall) | E opens a panel: every fish with its count; number keys or a click grab one; Z/X put one back. |
| **Pantry crates** (by the kitchen door) | Same panel for the sides (tortilla, rice, kelp, veggies, bread). |
| **Cutting board** (prep counter, back right) | E puts your ingredients on it (up to 4). When they match a known recipe, **HOLD SPACE** to chop (1.3 s). No-cook dishes are plated straight off the board. |
| **Grill / Fryer / Stove** | **HOLD SPACE** to put the chopped food on (0.8 s). It cooks for 6 s, stays Perfect for 3.5 s, Good for 5 s more, then burns. E takes it off (needs a clean plate). |
| **Sink** | E drops dirty plates in; **HOLD SPACE** washes one per 1 s (6% chance a plate breaks). |
| **Bin** (by the kitchen door) | Throw away the wrong thing. Ingredients go back in stock. |
| **Pass** (window to the dining room) | Line cooks leave finished dishes here; pick them up from either side. |

- Burners per station: 1, then 2, then 3 with the Kitchen upgrade.
- Ingredients for every booked order are set aside at opening; the panels show free stock plus what is set aside.
- Quality = cooking timing + fish freshness/cut (OK fish −0.12, Perfect cut +0.12, bruised −0.12). No-cook dishes score 0.75 before the fish modifiers.

## v4.1
- The chef's arms move while you HOLD: chopping, scrubbing at the sink, stirring at the heat. Steam rises from food that is cooking (dark smoke when it burns).
- **Sushi counter** (if built): HOLD to roll a no-cook dish for an always-Perfect result.
- **Kitchen lab** (prep panel): mix up to 4 ingredients to discover a recipe for free; a failed mix still uses them up.
