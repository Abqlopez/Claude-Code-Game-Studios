# Fishing (mornings)

**v4 (3D):** you sail a little **skiff** out from the anchored restaurant. Each fish shows its own 3D sign on the water ([[All Fish]] lists them). Bring the bow up to a sign and press SPACE to cast; the bobber flies out, and when the "!" appears press SPACE again within 1 s. A [[Mini Map]] shows spots, traps and home.
**v3 (2D):** the same rules on a top-down sea, steering the whole boat.

- Morning length: 90 s of real time (the clock slows during rare fights, see [[Fishing Days and Rarity]]).
- Bite wait: matching bait [0.5, 0.9] s, other bait [1.6, 3] s, no bait [2.2, 4] s (times the day's bite multiplier).
- Signs last 14-24 s (x1.4 in 3D, x the day's life multiplier), then a new one appears.
- Favourite bait makes the fish start the fight at 70% stamina. The Dragon Koi ignores anything but gold flakes.
- Wrong gear: a light rod snaps on big fish, a harpoon scares small fish, nets let big fish slip; a heavy rod on a small fish works but **bruises** it.
- Nets scoop 3-4 fish of a school at once (Sardine, Mackerel).
- Sizes: Small 25%, Normal 50%, Big 20% (feeds 2), Trophy 5% (feeds 3).
- A **clean catch** (no hits, tension never above 0.9) gives a Perfect cut that raises dish quality; 2+ hits bruise it.
- The tank holds 10 fish (15, then 25 with upgrades). Fish are fresh on day 1, OK on day 2, spoiled on day 3 (+1 day with a tank upgrade).
