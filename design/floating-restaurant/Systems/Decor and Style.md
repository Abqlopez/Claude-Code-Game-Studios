- Style stars: 2, 5, 8, 11, 14 points for 1-5 stars. Each star makes everyone 4% more patient; guests who love your theme get more and book more often.
- Themes: Beach Shack (loved by mermaid, crabfolk, family), Pirate Ship (loved by pirate, sailor, seawitch)
- Paints: Theme, Red, Navy, Gold, Mint, Plum ($25 each).
- v4: decor, paint and theme shopping are **not in 3D yet**; the plan is in [[Customizing in 3D]]. The 3D boat is built in the Beach Shack colours.

## In 3D (v4.1)
Decor is bought in the **Decor** tab and appears on the boat at once: Deck lanterns (and the night-fishing rod stand), Plants, the name Sign (an arch over the gangway), Checked tablecloths, Bunting, String lights, Rugs, and the Aquarium wall (with fish swimming in it). A new game starts with none of them.
- **Theme:** Beach Shack (cream hull, teal trim) or Pirate Ship (dark wood hull, red trim, darker deck). Free to switch.
- **Paint:** $25 to repaint the trim (hull band, rails, chairs, cabin).
- **Name:** type it in; it is painted on the stern and on the Sign.
