# Random events (v3 step 4)
One per day at most, from day 2; 25% of days are quiet.

| Event | When | Weight | What happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fish frenzy | morning | 25 | Rare fish are near the surface this morning. |
| Storm | night | 25 | Big waves shove you across the deck. Dirty plates left on empty tables slide off and smash. |
| Sea monster | night | 20 | A tentacle will grab an empty table. Hold E at the table to fight it off. |
| Rival food boat | night | 20 | Guests left waiting on the dock get lured away fast, so greet them quickly. |
| Celebrity Sea King | night | 15 | King Tidemane is coming. Only Perfect dishes will do, and his review counts 3 times. |

v4: the morning Fish frenzy works in 3D. Night events are rolled but **not played in 3D yet** (the Guests tab says so).

## In 3D (v4.1)
All four night events now happen in 3D:
- **Storm:** rain, a darker sky and a rocking boat. A warning ("◀ BIG WAVE!") comes before each wave; the wave shoves you across the deck and plates left on empty tables can slide off and smash. Lightning flashes now and then.
- **Sea monster:** a purple tentacle rises beside a free table and grabs it. HOLD E at the table to fight it off; the guests cheer and you get a reward.
- **Rival food boat:** the "RIVAL GRUB" boat bobs off the dock, and guests waiting in the queue lose patience much faster; any it lures away are a lost sale but not a bad review.
- **Celebrity Sea King:** King Tidemane arrives with a crown and a star over his head. Only Perfect dishes will do; his review counts 3 times.
The Guests tab and the night HUD show tonight's event.
