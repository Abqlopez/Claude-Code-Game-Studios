# Fishing days and rarity (v3.1)

Every morning rolls how good the fishing is (day 1 is always Normal):

| Day | Chance | Spots | Sign life | Bite wait | Respawn | Nothing bites |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Great (Fish are jumping!) | 15% | 6 | x1.3 | x0.8 | 0.9 s | 0% |
| Normal (Normal waters) | 50% | 5 | x1 | x1 | 0.9 s | 0% |
| Slow (Slow waters. The fish are not biting much.) | 25% | 3 | x0.8 | x1.7 | 6 s | 20% |
| Dead (Dead calm. Barely a nibble today.) | 10% | 2 | x0.7 | x2.3 | 10 s | 35% |

- **Daily shuffle:** every fish's chance is multiplied by a random 0.3-1.7 each morning, and one common/uncommon fish is "running" (x2.5).
- **[[Old Whiskers]]** is rare: from day 3, 12% of mornings; 4% after you have beaten him.
- **Slowed clock** while fighting: common 100%, uncommon 80%, rare 35%, legendary 30%, boss 20% of normal speed, so one big fish never eats the whole morning.
- The **Fish frenzy** event multiplies uncommon x2, rare x4, legendary x4.
