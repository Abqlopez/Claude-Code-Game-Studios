# Night service (v4.1)

The night has **no clock**: it ends when every booked guest has been served or has left. Guests arrive 3 s after opening, then 16 s apart shrinking to 8 s; the dock queue never holds more than 3.

## Flow
1. Guests walk down the dock and queue. **E at the podium** greets the next one: they **follow you**. Walk to a free, clean table (BIG for groups, VIP for Royals) and press **E** to seat them. While they follow you can still do everything else; if you go into the kitchen they wait by the door.
2. After 1.5 s they show their order: **E at the table** takes it (it goes on the ticket rail). They may ask a question (answer 1 or 2) or, now and then, ask for a dish that is not on the menu: put it on tomorrow's menu and they come back paying double.
3. Cook it in the [[Kitchen]] (or roll no-cook dishes at the **Sushi counter** for an always-Perfect dish).
4. **E at the table** serves it. Wrong dish or burnt = a **complaint** (apologise, free drink, remake). Royals take 3 courses one at a time.
5. They eat, then wave for the bill: **E at the table** collects it (late = no tip, and a star off). Crabfolk pay in shells. A Pirate may run for the dock: catch them before they reach it.
6. **E at the table** clears the plates (carry 2); drop them in the sink and **HOLD** to wash.

**Night fishing:** with the Deck lanterns you can fish off the bow (the rod stand by the rail) for a guest's "Catch of the moment", or for the tank. It needs the right bait, like the morning.

## Money per guest
Price x role pay x species pay x quality pay (Perfect 1.3, Good 1, Burnt 0.5); a requested dish pays double. Foodies pay nothing for a burnt dish and tip extra for Perfect.
Tip = 0.15 x that x patience left x combo bonus x bonuses (answered well, served a group together, a table they demanded).
Review = the worst dish's review, −1 if nearly out of patience, −1 for an ignored complaint; a walk-out is 1 star. A critic's review counts 5 times.
