# The boat (3D)

From front (bow) to back (stern):
- **Bow:** mast with a furled sail and waving flag, coiled rope, barrels, life ring. String lights run from the mast top to the lantern posts.
- **Dining room (open air):** 4 round tables with red-and-white striped tablecloths, plates, glasses and a candle jar; teal chairs (backs facing out); patterned rugs; striped awnings with scalloped edges along both sides; hanging lanterns; potted plants; host podium with a menu book and lamp; OPEN/CLOSED sign; "TONIGHT" chalkboard; gangplank gap in the railing.
- **Wall with a door and a pass window** (heat lamps, order tickets, bell), KITCHEN sign over the door.
- **Kitchen (open air, checkered tile):** grill (two fish, glowing coals, smoke), fryer (baskets, golden oil), stove (4 burners, steaming pot, pan with eggs), butcher-block prep counter with cutting board, knife and vegetables; sink and dish rack; fish tank with your catch swimming; produce crates (pantry); spice shelves; hanging pans; bin. Built staff stations appear here.
- **Bedroom cabin (behind the kitchen):** teal walls, red shingle roof with a chimney, square windows; bed with a patchwork quilt, nightstand lamp, desk with the fish journal and a candle, wardrobe, rug, trophy fish and ship's wheel on the wall. The roof and back wall fade when the chef is inside (and the roof fades while he works at the back of the kitchen).
- **Hull:** curved white hull, teal stripe, red bottom, brass portholes (glow at night), "Floating Restaurant" on the stern.
- **Around:** a dock with bollards, lamp post and barrels; the fishing **skiff** tied up alongside (outboard motor, cooler, tackle box, bow flag); islands with palm trees, clouds (hidden while fishing), gulls, a jumping fish.

Day/night: dawn, day, afternoon, dusk and night lighting; stars and moon at night.
