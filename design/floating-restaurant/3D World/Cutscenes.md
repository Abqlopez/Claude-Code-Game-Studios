# Cutscenes (v4.1)
| Scene | What happens |
|---|---|
| Wake up | The sun rises over the sea. The camera swoops into the cabin: the chef snores in a striped nightcap ("Zzz… mmm… fish tacos…"). The brass alarm clock rings; he jolts out of bed, walks over and SMACKS it, does a giant stretch and yawn, spins his nightcap off and his chef hat on ("CHEF MODE: ON!"), sniffs last night's broth in the kitchen, walks out and hops into the skiff. After day 1 the sunrise is shorter and the kitchen stop happens every other day. |
| Sea chart | Not a scene, a panel: see [[Areas]]. The skiff sails along a dotted route on the chart. |
| Afternoon | Golden light. The chef walks from the skiff to the kitchen tank and drops the catch in (or slumps if there is none). |
| Opening | Dusk. The chef walks to the podium, the OPEN sign flips, he cheers; lanterns, candles, string lights and portholes come on. |
| Closing | The chef flips the sign to CLOSED, walks to the desk in his cabin, sits, opens the journal and writes ("Dear journal…"). |
| Journal (results) | An open book: the left page is the morning (where, what the water was like, every fish caught with its picture and a NEW! stamp for first catches, morning goal, journal progress, spoiled fish); the right page is the night (served / walked out / stayed home, money lines counting up, profit with a PROFIT! stamp, rating and reviews, night goals, events, tomorrow's booking, the bait tomorrow's menu needs, SAVED stamp). A quill moves along as each line is written. "Finish writing" speeds it up. |
| Bed | He walks to the bed, yawns, puts the nightcap on, lies down, Z z z, fade to black. |

Every scene has a **Skip** button.
