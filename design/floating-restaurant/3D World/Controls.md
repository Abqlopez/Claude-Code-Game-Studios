# Controls (v4.1)
| Where | Keys | Touch / mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Walking | WASD or arrows | Click/tap the deck to walk there |
| Sea chart | 1, 2 to pick a sea; Enter/SPACE to set sail | Click a sea, then Set sail |
| Sailing the skiff | WASD or arrows | Click the sea to sail there; click a reachable sign to cast |
| Cast / hook | SPACE or E | Click the sign |
| Right bait for the sign in front of you | **R** | — |
| Gear / bait or lure / trap | 1-4 / Q / T | Buttons at the bottom left |
| Fight | Hold SPACE to reel; **A / D (or ◀ ▶) to pull against a run**; arrows/WASD for sparks, lure, reef; **tap SPACE in the green to land it** | Hold REEL; ◀ Pull / Pull ▶; arrow buttons |
| Night actions | E or SPACE (tap); HOLD to chop, load the heat, wash, roll sushi, fight a tentacle | Round E button (hold it to hold) |
| Questions and complaints | 1-4 to pick an answer | Click an answer |
| Fish tank / pantry panel | 1-9 grab, Z/X put back, E close | Click a slot |
| Build mode | — | Click a table, then click where it goes |
| Journal | SPACE/Enter finishes writing, then goes to bed | Finish writing / Go to bed |
| Camera | Z / X or +/- keys, mouse wheel | − / + buttons, pinch |
| Day/night preview | N | Night/Day button |
