# The day loop (v4.1)

1. **Title screen**: Continue or New game.
2. **Wake up** (scene): sunrise, snoring, the alarm, the stretch, the hat swap, out to the skiff. See [[Cutscenes]].
3. **Sea chart**: pick Home Waters, or Coral Bay with the engine. See [[Areas]].
4. **Morning fishing** (90 s): [[Fishing]], [[Gear and Bait]], [[Fish Fights]], [[Mini Map]]. The start of the morning says which fish your menu needs and how much bait you have for each.
5. **Catch summary** → "Sail back to the restaurant".
6. **Afternoon** (scene): the catch goes into the kitchen fish tank.
7. **Prep panel**: Guests, Menu, Shop, Gear + bait, Upgrades, Staff, Decor, Build, Kitchen lab, Journal → "Open for dinner".
8. **Opening** (scene): sunset, lanterns on, the sign flips to OPEN.
9. **[[Night Service]]** until every guest is served or gone. Some nights have an [[Events|event]].
10. **Closing** (scene): the sign flips to CLOSED, the chef walks to his desk and **writes the day in his journal** (the results). The day is saved.
11. **Bed** (scene): nightcap on, into bed, Z z z → next day.

Every scene has a **Skip** button.
