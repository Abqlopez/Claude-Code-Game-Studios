# v2
From the code comments of the 2D game:
- **Money rules:** every price derived from ingredient cost (3x), raw fish 40%, plates, journal rewards.
- **Fish fight v2:** stamina and line tension; reel while it is tired, ease off while it runs, dodge its special move.
- **Morning v2:** steer the boat around a top-down sea, read the signs on the water, pick gear and bait.
- **Version 2 art:** the walk-around deck, kitchen stations, the chef, carried items, the top-down sea, new fish and dishes.
- Gear (light rod, net, heavy rod, harpoon), baits, sizes, crab traps, fish freshness, the tank, the journal.
