# v4.1 (2026-11-16 to 2026-11-17) ([play](https://claude.ai/artifact/4nKDfuPaPCyDQQETW4CdNg))

The 3D game, finished: everything the 2D game had is now in 3D, plus a new morning and a new end of the day.

## Morning
| # | What | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **New wake-up scene** | Sunrise over the sea, the chef snoring in a nightcap, the alarm rings, he jumps out of bed, smacks the clock, yawns, swaps the nightcap for his chef hat, sniffs the pot, hops into the skiff. Shorter after day 1. See [[Cutscenes]]. |
| 2 | **Sea chart** | Picking your waters opens a hand-drawn chart. Click a sea, read its card (fish found, bait you have for it, guests), then Set sail. See [[Areas]]. |
| 3 | **Fights are less samey** | Moods, pulling ◀ ▶, a late extra trick, a landing timing bar, and Quick catch for fish you have mastered. See [[Fish Fights]]. |
| 5 | **Bait is the key** | Every fish has its own bait and its own lure. No bait, no bite. See [[Gear and Bait]]. |
| — | **Start with fish** | A new game starts with 3 Sardines and 3 Glowgills in the tank, and the bait for them, so day 1 can feed its guests. |

## Fixes after release
- Coming back from fishing, the chef no longer walks through the hull.
- While you lead a guest to a table you can still do everything else (clear tables, wash, cook, serve). Guests you lead wait by the kitchen door if you go inside.

## Choices made along the way
- **6 tables** = 2 more tables on a **dock patio**, not a longer boat.
- **Decor is bought**: a new game starts with a plainer boat (no lanterns, string lights, rugs or striped cloths until you buy them), like v3.
- **Item 4 (the menu vs. random fish)** was talked through, not built: see [[Roadmap]].

Testing: every part was played in a headless browser, including two full days through the real screens, a robot-run night with every guest type, each event, the hands-on night actions, decor and build mode, and phone-size screens. No errors.
