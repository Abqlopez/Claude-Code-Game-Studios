# v3 (built in 5 steps)
1. **Customer interaction:** chat lines, questions with a good and a bad answer, moods, complaints (apologise / free drink / remake), groups, troublemakers (pirates who dash, Sea Kings who demand a better table).
2. **New roles and species:** Family, Influencer, Critic (in disguise), Royal (3 courses); Octofolk, Crabfolk (shells), Sea Witch (blessings and curses).
3. **Upgrades that gate content, and style:** Big tables, VIP table, Dragon Grill, Boat engine; decor style points and stars; paint; themes loved by certain guests.
4. **Random events and daily goals:** frenzy, storm, sea monster, rival boat, celebrity Sea King; morning and night goals.
5. **Staff, layout and sections:** Host, Dishwasher, Waiter, Line Cook, Deckhand with training and moods; a layout editor to move tables and stations; Bar and Sushi counter; milestones.
- The night has no clock: it ends when every booked guest is served or gone.
