# v1: the design brief
The first plan ([[v1 Design Brief (original)|full text]]):
- Cozy fishing + cooking management, browser, desktop first.
- One small complete loop: 1 area, 5 fish, 7 recipes, 3 roles, 3 species, a short upgrade path, and sailing to a second area as the "you made it" moment.
- Every fish has its own reeling personality (Sardine schools, Glowgill blinking, Thunder Eel zaps, Bluefin dives, Dragon Koi spark patterns). A clean catch gives a Perfect cut.
- **Live catch:** guests order "catch of the moment" and you fish off the dining deck while they watch.
- Rule: if a feature isn't listed, it waits for version 2.
